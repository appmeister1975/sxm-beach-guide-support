SXM Beach Guide

Beach information and visitor-reported conditions for St. Maarten / Saint Martin.

App support

For questions, technical problems, or feedback, contact: sxmbeachapp@gmail.com

When reporting a technical issue, please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of what happened. Include the beach name if the issue concerns a condition report. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive account information.

Report incorrect information

To report inaccurate beach information or concerns about a visitor report, email us with the beach name, the approximate report time, and a description of the concern.

About condition reports

Visitors can report beach sargassum, water sargassum, waves, and weather. Recent reports are used to summarize conditions in the app.

Visitor reports may be incomplete, inaccurate, or out of date. Map colors are not swimming-safety ratings. Follow posted warnings and local guidance. This support email is not an emergency service.

Privacy questions

For questions about your information or the app’s privacy practices, contact sxmbeachapp@gmail.com .
