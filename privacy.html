SXM Beach Guide Privacy Policy Effective date: November 17, 2026 SXM Beach Guide lets you browse beaches in St. Maarten and Saint Martin and view and share recent beach conditions. This policy explains what information the app uses, how long it is kept, and how to delete your guest account and reports. Information the app uses You can browse the beach guide without an account. To view live community reports or submit a report, you can start a guest account. The app uses Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous account with a unique guest ID. You don’t need to provide a name, email address, or password to start a guest account. When you submit a report, the app stores the beach, the submission time, your guest ID, and your ratings for sargassum on the beach, sargassum in the water, waves, and weather. The app does not upload photos with reports. The app also stores a record of your most recent submission for each beach where you report. It uses this record to enforce a five-minute wait between reports from the same guest account for the same beach. How information is used and shared Reports are shared with other app users to help them see recent beach conditions. The app uses your guest ID to associate your reports with your account, enforce the reporting limit, and find your reports if you request deletion. The app uses Google Firebase Authentication to manage guest accounts and Cloud Firestore to store reports and related records. If you email support, your email address and any information you include are used to respond to and handle your request. Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore collect diagnostic information for analytics about how those services operate. This diagnostic information is not linked to your guest account and is not used for tracking. How long information is kept The app uses reports from the past four hours to show recent conditions. A report falling outside that window is no longer used to show current conditions, but it is not automatically deleted at that point. Reports become eligible for routine cleanup after 24 hours. Cleanup runs on a best-effort basis while the app is in use and connected. It does not run on a guaranteed daily schedule, so reports may remain stored longer. Routine report cleanup does not delete guest accounts or their submission-limit records. How to delete your guest account and reports In the app, open Privacy & Support → Delete Guest Account & Reports and confirm. When deletion completes, the app removes your remaining reports, your submission-limit records, and your Firebase guest account. Your deleted reports are no longer available to other users through the app. If deletion is interrupted, reopen Privacy & Support and select Resume Deletion when you’re back online. A limited deletion record containing the former guest ID and the time deletion began remains after the account is deleted. It is used to prevent the former guest account from submitting reports again. This record does not contain your reports and is not an active guest account. It is not currently removed on an automatic schedule. Contact For privacy questions or help completing a deletion request, email sxmbeachapp@gmail.com.
